Privacy Policy

Quran & Science: Proofs (the “app”) · Urdushev Studio
Effective 1 October 2026

This app shows verses of the Quran next to the scientific discoveries and historical records they are compared with. We built it to be read, not to watch you. We do not run a server, we do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect, store or sell your personal data. This page explains the few places where data does leave your phone, and why.

1. What stays on your phone

  • Your settings (translation language, Arabic text size, light or dark theme) and the facts you bookmark.
  • A subscription flag (whether Google Play confirmed your ad-free subscription) and a small counter used to decide how often an ad may appear.
  • Pictures you choose to share are created temporarily in the app's cache and handed to the app you pick.

None of this is sent to us. Uninstalling the app erases it.

2. Advertising (free version)

The free version shows an occasional full-screen ad, provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process your advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app first shows Google's consent form and only requests ads according to your choice. You can change your choice at any time under More → Privacy choices where it is offered.

  • Ads are limited to the “G” (general audiences) content rating.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Subscribers do not see ads, and the advertising software is not started for them.

3. Subscriptions and payments

The ad-free subscription is sold and billed by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. Google tells the app only whether your subscription is active. You can manage or cancel it at any time in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.

4. Pictures, video and recitation

To display photographs and video, the app loads files from Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org). Verse recitation audio is loaded from the Islamic Network content network (cdn.islamic.network). Like any website or server you connect to, these services can see your IP address and the files you request. We do not send them anything else, and we do not control their logs. Each photo and video shows its author and licence.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and is not part of Google Play's Families programme. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

7. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data held by Google (ads, payments) you can use the controls in your Google Account and Android settings, and you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR and the UK GDPR, including to withdraw consent, which you can do in the app as described above.

8. Security

All network connections made by the app use HTTPS.

9. Changes

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its effective date. The current version is always at this address.
